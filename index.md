### My scientific journey

Conny obtained her PhD from [CUHK](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html){:target="_blank"} (Hong Kong). Following postdoctoral training at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} (London) and [St Andrews](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} (Fife), she joined the [MRC-LMB](https://mrclmb.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} (Cambridge) as a Staff Scientist. She has collaborated widely and published across multiple fields, including cell division, RNA transcription and protein translation.

Leaving the laboratory behind, she transitioned into data science and joined [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} (Cambridge) as a Data Curator. She specialised in large-scale data curation and cross-database integration, contributing to the data harmonisation across UniProt, PDB and the AlphaFold Database. Currently working as a Data Curation Consultant, she is particularly interested in building high-quality data infrastructure that accelerates biological discovery.
